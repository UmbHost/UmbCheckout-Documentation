{% hint style="warning" %}
**Perpetual V1 licenses.** The V1 license is an annual subscription, with updates and support included until 31 December 2026, when Umbraco 13 reaches end of life. A V1 license bought as a one-off purchase before 14 October 2026 is a "perpetual" license. It keeps working with UmbCheckout versions before 1.6.0. To use 1.6.0 or later, convert it to an annual V1 license in [UmbPanel](https://www.umbpanel.io). From 1.6.0 a perpetual key shows the status "This licence doesn't cover UmbCheckout 1.6 or later: convert it to an annual licence", and the licensed add-ons stop until it is converted. The open-source core and your stored data are not affected.

A license is included with hosting on [GreenStack](https://umbhost.net) for Umbraco 10, 13 and 17. It is only valid for sites hosted on GreenStack and ends with the hosting.
{% endhint %}
